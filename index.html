About

I obtained my PhD in 2026 from Virginia Tech under the supervision of Eric Sharpe, and am now a postdoc in the Mathematical Structures in Physics group led by Daniel Roggenkamp at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences (MPI MiS) in Leipzig.

I am interested in stating physical structures, such as symmetries and their anomalies, precisely enough to prove theorems about them, and in letting that precision suggest new physics and new mathematics.

Research

  • Generalized symmetries

    Higher-form and non-invertible symmetries of quantum field theories, their gauging, and their anomalies.

  • Symmetry TFTs

    Using SymTFTs and multi-fusion categories to organise the symmetry data of a theory.

  • Decomposition

    How theories with certain symmetries split into sums of simpler universes.

  • Higher geometry

    Cohesive and higher-categorical methods for gauge theory and sigma models.

Miscellaneous

Outside physics, I am interested in philosophy and theology, above all Aristotle, Saint Thomas Aquinas, Søren Kierkegaard, and Joseph Ratzinger. The topics that draw me most are natural philosophy and ethics in the Aristotelian–Thomistic tradition, the relation between faith and reason, and the philosophy of mathematics and its bearing on physical reality.

I am also drawn to Christian mystical theology, particularly Saint John of the Cross, read through Saint Thomas as Réginald Garrigou-Lagrange does, and Saint Edith Stein, whose work brings phenomenology and Thomism into conversation with the Carmelite tradition. More broadly, I am interested in the different apostolic Christian traditions, especially the Latin and the Syriac: what the Syriac Fathers, from Saint Ephrem to Saint Isaac of Nineveh, have to offer alongside the Latin inheritance of Saint Augustine and Saint Thomas.

I am curious, too, about other religious traditions and how they meet Christianity, from Zoroastrianism and Manichaeism to Islam, Jewish mysticism, Daoism, and the thought of Islamic philosophers such as Ibn ʿArabi, Ibn Rushd, and Ibn Sina. Closer to home, I am drawn to devotion to Our Lady and to the Blessed Sacrament, and to their place in the interior life.
